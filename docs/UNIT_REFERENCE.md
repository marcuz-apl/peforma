# Unit Converter

Peforma Units converts ten engineering categories without sending anything to a server. Linear units are defined by an exact factor to the category base unit; temperature uses offset formulas because its scales do not share a zero point.

## Definitions

Ton means the metric tonne (1,000 kg), not the US short ton or UK long ton.

Month is the mean Gregorian month, one twelfth of a 365.2425-day year; quarter is three months, and decade and century are ten and one hundred years on the same basis.

Barrel is the 42 US gallon oil barrel. ppg is pound per US gallon. Atmosphere is the standard atmosphere. lb·ft is the pound-force foot.

US gallon is 3.785411784 liters; UK gallon is the imperial gallon of 4.54609 liters.

## Display

Results are shown to at most 10 significant digits with grouping. Values at or above 10¹⁵ or below 10⁻⁹ use six-decimal exponential notation. Empty or non-numeric input shows an em dash rather than a guessed value.
